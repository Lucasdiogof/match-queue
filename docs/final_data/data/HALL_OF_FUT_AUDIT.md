# Hall of FUT rating audit

As of 2026-09-08, 20 of the 21 Hall of FUT players have the same rating in
FCData's reveal article and its live/latest database listing.

One discrepancy is preserved explicitly instead of being silently resolved:

- Giovani dos Santos: reveal article = 84; live FCData listing = 80.

Do not overwrite one with the other without checking the post-10/09 database refresh.
